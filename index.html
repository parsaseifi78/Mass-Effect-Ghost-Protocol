Mass Effect Ghost Protocol.

A fan-made 3D sequel to Mass Effect Infiltrator: Resurrection. Swipe biotics, cinematic bosses, a silent tactical HUD, and a fan-written story — built locally for modern Android, with no external assets.

Randall returns

Swipe-only biotics

Drag the top-left circle onto a visible arc slot and release. The button itself never casts, and the three-slot wheel eases open before settling.

Silent vitals

No health or shield numbers. Bars lose colour saturation toward neutral steel as they fall, staying bright enough to read in combat.

Health before shields

150 health with regeneration starting one second after damage; shields wait three. The objective, morality, and vitals tiles share the minimap's height.

Cinematic escalation

Praetorian, Lancer, Juggernaut, and Director stages get their own entrances, abilities, cutscenes, and mission hand-offs.

Local multiplayer

Two-player co-op and PVP with FFA or Team A vs B. Choose one limited-use ability and one weapon before the round starts.

Phone-first renderer

Native Choreographer pacing with rAF fallback, 60/120 Hz snapping, capped DPR, no shadows, and adaptive render scale.

Install

From the phone

Copy the APK from /sdcard/Download/Ghost Protocol/ and open it. The package is com.ghost.protocol, so it installs alongside the Resurrection build.

From source

bash tools/build_apk.sh \
  "/sdcard/Download/Ghost Protocol/Local.apk"

The script assembles smali, packages assets, aligns, and signs with the project key.

Controls at a glance

GitHub picker

Tap the top-left circle/triangle on the title screen, or press G, to choose between the Resurrection and Ghost Protocol repositories.

Biotics

Drag the top-left circle onto a slot and release. Keyboard: E or 1–3.

Cloak

Bottom-right button, permanently mapped. Keyboard: C.

Exit

Title-screen × or Esc closes the app through the native bridge.
